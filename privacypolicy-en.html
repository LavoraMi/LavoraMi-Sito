LavoraMi Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data of users (hereinafter "Users" or "Data Subjects") who use the LavoraMi mobile application (hereinafter the "App"), available on iOS and Android platforms, is collected, used, and protected.

Data processing is carried out in accordance with the fundamental rights and freedoms, as well as the dignity, of the data subject, with particular reference to confidentiality, personal identity, and the right to the protection of personal data, in compliance with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable Italian law.

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller (the party who decides how and why your data is processed) is:

  • Name / Business Name: Simone Gallotti
  • Registered Address: Milan
  • Contact Email: info@lavorami.it

2. Types of Data Collected

The App collects several categories of data to ensure the service functions properly and to improve its performance.

2.1 Data provided voluntarily by the User (Account Data)

To create an account and access personalized features, we collect:

  • Name: To identify the user within the App.
  • Email Address: Used as a unique identifier for login, for service communications, or for credential recovery.
  • Password: Created by the user. Important note: The password is immediately encrypted (via hashing) and stored in protected form. The Data Controller never has access to the User's password in plain text.

2.2 Preference and Personalization Data

To provide a personalized experience and synchronization across devices, the App collects and stores on our servers:

  • Favorite Lines: The lines the User marks as favorites (e.g. to receive targeted notifications).
  • Your Lines: The lines selected by the User for the "Your Lines" filter on the main screen.

User control over synchronization: Within the App, in the "Account" → "Data Preferences" section, the User can independently decide which of the following data categories they wish to synchronize with the servers:

  • Favorite Lines;
  • "Your Lines".

Each category can be individually enabled or disabled at any time. Data related to disabled categories is not sent to or stored on the servers and remains exclusively on the User's device.

This data is uniquely associated with the User's email address to ensure correct synchronization across multiple sessions and devices, not with the technical session ID (which is temporary and variable).

2.3 Data collected through advertising services (Google AdMob)

The App integrates the Google AdMob SDK to display advertisements. Even though LavoraMi does not actively collect data itself, Google's SDK may automatically collect the following information:

  • The device's Advertising ID (Android or iOS);
  • Approximate location data (if the user has granted location permissions);
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks, conversions);
  • Device data (model, operating system, App version, language, screen resolution);
  • IP address (anonymized, for security and measurement purposes).

This data is used by Google to provide personalized advertising (based on interests) and to measure campaign effectiveness. Google acts as data processor on behalf of the Data Controller, under the terms of the Google AdMob service agreement.

The User can disable ad personalization at any time through their device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization;
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking (for iOS 14+).

2.4 Data collected through Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring

The App integrates Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring, services provided by Google LLC, to understand aggregate App usage and monitor its technical performance. These tools automatically collect:

  • Usage data: App opens, session length, usage frequency (daily/weekly/monthly active users);
  • Device data: Model, operating system, App version, language, installation identifier (not directly traceable to the User's identity);
  • Performance data: App startup times, network request latency to our servers, any slowdowns or technical errors.

This data is collected in aggregate, statistical form and is not used to individually identify the User or for advertising purposes. Google acts as data processor on behalf of the Data Controller.

For users residing in the EEA/UK, data collection via Firebase Analytics is subject to consent, managed through the same consent collection tool (Google UMP) used for advertising, also accessible later from the App settings.

3. Purposes of Processing and Legal Basis

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision (Account Management):
    • Purpose: To allow registration, authentication, and use of the App's features (e.g. viewing ongoing works, saving favorite lines).
    • Legal Basis: Performance of a contract to which the data subject is party (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Security and Maintenance:
    • Purpose: To ensure App security, prevent fraud, and restore access in case of lost credentials.
    • Legal Basis: Legitimate interest of the Data Controller (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
  • Personalization and synchronization:
    • Purpose: To save and synchronize only the data categories (favorite lines and/or "your lines") that the User has actively chosen to share via the "Data Preferences" interface. Categories not selected remain local to the device and are not stored in the Database.
    • Legal Basis: Performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR) – personalization is a feature requested by the User.
  • Personalized advertising (Google AdMob):
    • Purpose: To show ads relevant to the User's interests and to fund the service.
    • Legal Basis: Explicit consent of the User (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) for users residing in the EEA/UK. For users outside the EEA, processing is based on Google's legitimate interest.
  • Statistical analysis and performance monitoring:
    • Purpose: To understand aggregate App usage (e.g. number of active users) and identify technical or performance issues in order to improve the Service.
    • Legal Basis: Explicit consent of the User (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) for users residing in the EEA/UK with regard to Analytics; legitimate interest of the Data Controller (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) for Performance Monitoring, limited to non-identifying technical data.

4. Processing Methods and Security Measures

Processing is carried out using IT and telematic tools, following logic strictly related to the purposes indicated.

The Data Controller adopts appropriate technical and organizational security measures to prevent data loss, unlawful or improper use, and unauthorized access. In particular:

  • Passwords are stored exclusively in encrypted (hashed) form.
  • Communications between the App and the servers take place via secure protocols (SSL/HTTPS).
  • Access to the databases is restricted exclusively to authorized personnel.

5. Data Retention Period

Data is retained for the time strictly necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected.

  • Account and preference data (name, email, password, favorite lines, "your lines"): Retained until an account deletion request is made. Upon deletion, all associated personal data, including preferences, is deleted within 5 days, subject to legal obligations.
  • Data collected by AdMob: Retained by Google according to its own retention policies (generally no more than 13 months for interaction data).
  • Data collected by Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring: Retained by Google according to its default policies (generally 2 months for user-level data, unless otherwise configured by the Data Controller in the Firebase Console).

Data related to categories for which the User has disabled synchronization is never transmitted to the servers, and is therefore not subject to centralized retention; it is managed only locally on the device.

6. Data Recipients

Personal data will not be disclosed to the public. It may be shared with:

  • Technical service providers: Parties that provide server hosting, database, or analytics services (e.g. Google, Apple, cloud providers), appointed as Data Processors when they process personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.
  • Google (AdMob): Data collected by the advertising SDK is shared with Google and its advertising partners, according to Google's terms of service.
  • Google Firebase: Usage and performance data collected via Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring is processed by Google LLC as Data Processor, under the terms of the Firebase Data Processing Terms.
  • Competent authorities: Only when required by law or to protect the rights of the Data Controller.

7. Data Transfers Outside the EU

The App may use technology services (e.g. cloud servers) located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). In such cases, the Data Controller ensures that the transfer takes place in compliance with Articles 44 et seq. of the GDPR, based on Adequacy Decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission.

8. User Rights (GDPR)

As a data subject, Users residing in the European Union have the right to exercise, at any time, the rights set out in Articles 15-22 of the GDPR:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation of the existence of personal data and obtain a copy of it.
  • Right to Rectification: Request the updating or correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the deletion of personal data when it is no longer necessary or in case of withdrawal of consent.
  • Right to Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain cases.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest, including processing for direct marketing purposes (advertising profiling).

How to exercise your rights

To exercise the above rights, the User can send a written request to the email address: info@lavorami.it. The Data Controller will respond to requests as soon as possible and within 20 business days.

The User also has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

Please note that the right to portability applies exclusively to personal data that has actually been subject to automated processing with the User's consent or in performance of a contract. Data that was never transmitted to the servers (because the User disabled synchronization for that category) is not covered by portability, as it is not available to the Data Controller.

9. Account Deletion

The User can request the deletion of their account directly within the application via the appropriate function in "Settings", through the website www.lavorami.it/account/login by logging into their account, or by sending an email to the Data Controller. Deleting the account results in the permanent removal of the associated personal data (Name, Email).

Note for the User: Installing and using the App constitutes acknowledgment of this Privacy Policy.
